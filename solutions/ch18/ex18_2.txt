How the Policy Assistant uses your information
What it does: answers questions about IT policies using only our approved policy documents.
What we process: your question, your staff ID and your department (to show only policies you
are allowed to see). We do not use your questions to train AI models.
Who processes it: our IT team and [provider], which processes questions on our behalf under a
contract that prohibits training on our data.
How long: questions are kept for 30 days for troubleshooting, then deleted. Anonymous counts
are kept for reporting.
AI limits: answers may be wrong. Always check the linked policy before acting.
Your choices: you can ask a person instead at the IT service desk, and you can request access to
or deletion of your records through privacy@company.example.
