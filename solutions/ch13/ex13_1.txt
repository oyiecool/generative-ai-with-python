Decision: Use RAG over the HR policy collection with a mid-tier hosted model. Do not fine-tune.
Context: Policies change several times a year; answers must cite the current version;
         access differs between managers and staff.
Options considered:
  - Prompting only: fails, the model does not know our policies.
  - Fine-tuning: stores facts that go stale; no citations; no per-user access control.
  - RAG: current, citable, filterable by permission.  ← chosen
Consequences: Need an ingestion pipeline and document owners; revisit fine-tuning only if
              response style or cost becomes a problem at scale.
