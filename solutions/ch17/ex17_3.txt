AI Tools Acceptable Use — Infrastructure Team (v1.0)
1. Approved tools: [list]. Request others through the service desk; do not sign up personally.
2. Never enter: credentials, keys, customer personal data, unreleased vulnerability details,
   or confidential source code into any tool not approved for that data class.
3. You remain responsible for anything you submit, send or deploy that AI helped produce.
   Verify commands, configuration and facts before use; never run generated commands on
   production without review.
4. Disclose AI assistance when sharing work externally or when a reviewer asks.
5. Report wrong, harmful or surprising outputs to the AI governance lead.
6. Breaches are handled under the information security policy.
Owner: AI governance lead. Review: every 6 months.
